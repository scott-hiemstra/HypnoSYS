# ⚠️ Read this before you play HYPNOSYS

HYPNOSYS is an interactive art piece that imitates a hypnotic induction. It
uses slowly rotating spirals, a narrowing field of view, warping visuals,
mirrored controls, and a binaural-beat drone, all designed to be absorbing
and disorienting.

## Do not run it at all if you

- **have epilepsy, have ever had a seizure, or are sensitive to moving
  patterns or light.** The visuals move slowly and nothing flashes, but
  rotating spirals can still be a trigger.
- **have a history of psychosis, schizophrenia, dissociative disorders, or
  any other serious mental health condition, or are in crisis right now.**
  The game deliberately blurs the line between you and what's on the screen.
- **are under the influence of alcohol or drugs.**
- **are driving, operating machinery, or anywhere you need to stay alert.**
- **are a child.** This is not made for children.

## While you play

- **Stop immediately** if you feel dizzy, nauseous, anxious, disoriented,
  or unwell in any way. Press **Esc** at any time to be brought out gently
  (v3 skips to the end, where your eyes open), or just close the window.
- Keep the volume moderate. The drone is meant to sit in the background.

## Afterwards

Sit for a few minutes before you stand up, and don't drive straight away.

HYPNOSYS is entertainment. It is not therapy, it is not a medical device,
and it makes no claim to treat, change, or improve anything.
